Questions?

What are tokens?

Tokens are how AI measures usage. ~10,000 tokens is about 7,500 words of conversation - enough for 100-200 calls per month, depending on how much you chat.

Is this really private?

Yes. We don't build profiles on you. We don't sell your data. Your conversations are encrypted and never used to train AI models. That's our promise - and it's patent pending.

Can I switch between AIs?

Absolutely! You can change your AI provider anytime in settings. Want ChatGPT today and Grok tomorrow? No problem.

What if I run out of tokens?

You'll get a notification when you're close. You can upgrade to a higher plan or wait until next month. We'll have higher-tier plans available soon.

Can I pick my own number?

Yes! When it's your turn, you'll get to choose from available numbers. We want this to feel personal.

How does memory work?

Ghost AI remembers what you tell it across calls. Ask it to remember something, and it will. Later, ask "what did I tell you about X?" and it recalls.
